About Asistora

Building the future of intelligent automation, one conversation at a time.

Our Mission

To simplify communication through AI-powered chatbot solutions that provide instant, smart, and efficient support for businesses, educational institutions, and digital platforms.

Our Vision

To become a leading AI chatbot platform that transforms how organizations connect, support, and engage with people through intelligent automation.

We aim to make intelligent, accessible automation available to every team that needs it.

The Story Behind Asistora

From frustration to innovation.

Founded by a team of AI researchers and serial entrepreneurs who were tired of clunky chatbots that failed to deliver real value.


Assistora started as a university project with the goal of improving communication through AI. What began as an idea for educational support soon expanded into a broader vision of creating smart chatbot solutions for industries like eCommerce, customer service, and digital businesses. Today, Assistora focuses on building intelligent, modern, and accessible AI assistants for the future.
